Privacy Policy: Wise Owl Uploader

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Wise Owl Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Wise Owl Retirement to manage that channel and its matching TikTok account and Facebook Page. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails, and captions to the owner's own YouTube channel, and manage those videos' details, playlists, and publish schedules;
  • post and read comments on the owner's own videos;
  • read the owner's own channel analytics (YouTube Analytics).

When the owner connects TikTok, the app requests permission to read the owner's basic TikTok profile and to upload or publish the owner's own videos to that TikTok account.

When the owner connects Facebook, the app requests permission to list the Pages the owner manages, publish and schedule videos and Reels on the owner's own Wise Owl Retirement Page, and read and moderate (hide or reply to) comments on that Page's posts.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to operate the owner's own YouTube channel, TikTok account, and Facebook Page.
  • Sign-in credentials and any data retrieved are stored only on the owner's personal computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app does not access any other person's Google account or YouTube data.

Google API Services, TikTok, and Meta

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service , Google Privacy Policy, TikTok Privacy Policy, and Meta Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, or in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.

Deleting your data

The app stores no data about anyone other than the owner. To delete the app's stored sign-in credentials, remove the app's access as described above (Google, TikTok, or Facebook Business integrations); the owner also deletes the local credential files. Any other data deletion request can be sent through the Wise Owl Retirement YouTube channel and will be handled within 30 days.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Wise Owl Retirement YouTube channel.
